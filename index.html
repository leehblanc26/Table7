The next-ball preview updates immediately after a drop; inputs are ignored during cascades. Force the next ball to test a situation. Forced balls are excluded from random-distribution statistics. Select “Random” to resume ordinary play.

Generated numbered-ball counts (1/2: 10% each; 3–7: 16% each):

Rules in this prototype: New games begin with 7–21 randomly placed numbered balls, with up to four per column and empty columns permitted. A complete clear awards 1,000 bonus points (provisional). A numbered ball clears when its value equals the length of a contiguous occupied run horizontally or vertically. Clears can expose hidden balls, gravity can cause more clears, and a new row rises every five drops. Hidden balls take two adjacent clearing events to reveal their preassigned number. Each newly rising row contains seven fully concealed white balls with independently preassigned random values. Break Shot: arm or cancel using the rack icon; spend one available charge to drop an 8 ball into a column. It clears all eight neighboring positions and itself for zero points; subsequent matches score normally. Undo restores the pre-shot state. Scoring remains provisional pending original-game verification. All matches eligible on the same board snapshot clear together; gravity occurs afterward.
