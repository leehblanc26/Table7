How to Play Table 7

Drop and clear

Tap a column to drop the next numbered ball into the 7×7 table. A numbered ball clears when its number equals the length of its connected horizontal or vertical line of occupied spaces. All matching balls clear together; falling balls can trigger more clears.

Mystery balls

White cue balls conceal numbers. Adjacent clears crack them, then reveal their numbers after another hit. A new row of seven mystery balls rises every five drops.

Break Shot

Tap the 🎱 button to arm or cancel your Break Shot, then tap a column to drop the 8-ball. It removes itself and occupied neighboring spaces, including diagonals. You start with one shot and can earn more with a qualifying TABLE RUN! Unused shots stack.

Scoring

Each normally cleared ball earns 10 points × cascade level (first clear ×1, next ×2, and so on). Direct Break Shot removals score zero, but subsequent ordinary clears score normally. Clearing the entire board without using a Break Shot in that move earns a 1,000-point TABLE RUN! bonus and one additional Break Shot. Break Shot moves and their cascades cannot earn TABLE RUN! awards.

End of game

The game ends when the table fills. Aim for a high score, or use Concede to end early.
